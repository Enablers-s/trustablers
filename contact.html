Questions

Before you get in touch.

What happens after I send an enquiry?

A member of our legal team reviews it and contacts you to understand the matter, confirm the scope and timing, and explain the next steps.

Do you work with companies outside the UAE?

Yes. Cross-border work is a core part of what we do, from contracts with overseas counterparties to structuring across several markets.

Which languages can you draft contracts in?

We draft in the languages your counterparties work in, English and Arabic most often. Tell us the languages you need when you get in touch.

Is a contract drafted with AI reliable?

AI prepares the first draft and keeps language versions consistent. A legal professional then reviews every document before it reaches you, and nothing is sent for signature without that review.

Is what I share kept confidential?

Yes. Information about your matter stays with the team handling it.

Is the information on this website legal advice?

No. It is general information only. Advice on your situation starts once we have confirmed an engagement with you in writing.
