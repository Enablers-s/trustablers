About the firm

A legal team that already knows your file.

Trustablers is the corporate law firm of the Enabler-S group, based in Dubai and working with businesses in the UAE and across borders.

Most businesses don’t need a lawyer once. They need one who stays with them, from the first contract to the group structure.

Trustablers was set up inside Enabler-S for exactly that. Companies that license, bank and hire through the group were asking for legal support from people who already understood their business. So we built a corporate legal practice around one idea: one team holds your documents, deadlines and history, and nothing has to be explained twice.

We combine AI drafting with human legal review. AI gets the first draft and every language version done quickly and consistently; our legal professionals check every document before it reaches you. You get speed without giving up care.

Our principles

How we practise.

  • Clarity

  • Confidentiality
